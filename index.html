Gout-Friendly Kitchen Reference

Freely Enjoy

  • Chicken and turkey — lower purine, great for most nights
  • Eggs — gout-neutral, solid protein
  • Low-fat dairy — cheese, cream, and sour cream are all fine
  • Vegetables — peppers, onions, corn, beans, leafy greens
  • Rice, tortillas, and pasta — complex carbs are your friend
  • Tart cherry juice — shown to reduce flare frequency
  • Water with every meal — helps flush uric acid
  • Olive oil, garlic, fresh herbs — cook with them freely

Enjoy in Moderation

  • Beef and pork — keep to 4–6 oz portions, not every single night
  • Seafood — love it, just keep to ~4 oz per person, 1–2x per week
  • White fish (cod, tilapia) — lower purine than shrimp, great option
  • Beer and liquor — major gout trigger, go easy
  • Store-bought sauces — often have HFCS; make from scratch when possible
  • Anchovies and sardines — skip these specifically
  • Organ meats — skip entirely
  • Sugary sodas — swap for water or low-sugar drinks at dinner
